Takeaway & Delivery

You can now experience the convenience and quality of La Pazza Gioia Takeaway & Delivery at Chaussée de Wavre 314, Elsene - Brussels

This location is dedicated to serving you delicious, authentic Italian food for easy pick-up or home delivery. Our menu features a selection of highly popular items, including:

  • Artisan Pizzas
  • Authentic Panuozzi (Italian Sandwiches)
  • Freshly Prepared Pastas
  • Signature Desserts

Enjoy your meal at home by ordering through our convenient delivery partners:

  • Takeaway.com
  • Deliveroo
  • UberEats
